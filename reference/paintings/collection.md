## Decisions (2026-11-19)

- Placement (Maria): left wall in walking order Mucha → Waterhouse → Hokusai; right wall
  Klimt → Van Gogh → Kandinsky (the nave's three bays, facing in pairs); the Bosch open,
  standing in the far doorway under the last arch, on a low base.
- Hiroshige and Titian (added later the same day): on the crossing's side walls
  under the dome, Titian left, Hiroshige right (Maria switched them from my provisional placing).
- Then Kandinsky and Hiroshige swapped (Maria): Hiroshige in the nave's third bay on the right,
  Kandinsky under the dome on the right.
- Klimt kept with the book scan; its zoom is capped at 1 screen pixel per image pixel.
- Hylas hung at 98.2 × 163.3 cm: the image's proportions (0.600) match it, not
  132.1 × 197.5 (0.669, likely framed). Still to confirm against Manchester's record.
- Crops (original pixels): Mucha to the paper sheet 268,442–6796,12936 (0.523 vs BnF 0.519);
  Kandinsky inside the frame 47,110–3872,2765 (0.694 vs 0.697); Klimt inside the book page
  90,174–5886,3378 (0.553 vs 0.552); Bosch outer black rim trimmed, resized to 12000 px wide.

## Fit in the hall (70 %)

- A nave bay is 2.38 m wide between pilasters; second-arm bays about 3.4 m; the crossing's
  closed side walls are 7.7 m long under the dome.
- Only the Garden doesn't fit a nave bay (3.26 m open). Kandinsky (2.01 m) fits with little
  margin.
